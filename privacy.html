Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

This policy explains what information Yoder Performance 4x4 (“we”, “us”), a vehicle service and repair business, collects through our mobile apps and text-message program, how it is used, and your choices.

Information we collect

  • Account information — your name, email address, phone number, and service address.
  • Vehicle information — the vehicles you add (year/make/model, VIN, mileage, and any photos you attach).
  • Service information — appointments, estimates, inspections, invoices, and messages related to your service.
  • Payment information — deposits and invoices are processed by Stripe. Your full card number is entered on Stripe’s secure checkout and is not stored by us. We retain a record that a payment was made and the amount.

We do not collect the customer’s device location. When your mechanic is en route, the app shows the mechanic’s shared location so you can see their arrival — it does not track you.

Text messaging (SMS)

If you provide your mobile number and opt in, we may send you SMS text messages for appointment reminders and confirmations, estimates ready for approval, invoices and receipts, and service updates about your vehicle.

  • Message frequency varies based on your service activity.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP at any time to unsubscribe. Reply HELP for help, or contact us using the details below.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
Your mobile opt-in is never shared for marketing. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties.

How we use your information

  • To schedule and perform vehicle service and keep your service history.
  • To prepare and send estimates and invoices, and to process payments.
  • To contact you about your appointments and service (by app notification, email, or SMS).
  • To operate, secure, and improve our apps.

We do not sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

We share data only with the service providers (“sub-processors”) that make our apps work. Each processes data only to provide its service to us and, as stated above, none receive your SMS opt-in data for their own marketing. We do not sell your personal information.

  • Supabase — secure hosting and database for all app data.
  • Stripe — payment processing for deposits and invoices.
  • Google Workspace (Gmail) — sending you email (confirmations, estimates, receipts).
  • Twilio — sending SMS text messages, if you opt in.
  • Google Firebase — delivering push notifications to your device.
  • Google Maps — mapping and routing for service addresses.
  • Google Calendar — syncing appointments, only if we connect a calendar.
  • Anthropic (Claude) — an AI service that helps us prepare repair estimates and may process vehicle and service details. It is not used to sell or profile you, and (per Anthropic’s terms) your data is not used to train their models.
  • Legal — we may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights.

Data retention

We keep your account and service records while your account is active and as needed to provide service. Some financial records (such as invoices) may be retained after account deletion where required for tax, warranty, or legal purposes; these are disassociated from your personal profile.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from Settings → Delete my account in the app. This removes your login and erases your personal profile (name, email, phone, address), subject to the records noted under “Data retention.”

Security

We use industry-standard measures (encrypted connections, access controls, and row-level security) to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

Children

Our apps are not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We may update this policy; the “Last updated” date will change. Continued use means you accept the updated policy.
